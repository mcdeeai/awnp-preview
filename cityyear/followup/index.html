How It Works

We Produce. You Share.

Applied Wisdom handles all production. City Year gets a co-branded landing page with the full asset suite for each episode we produce together, plus access to the complete 66-episode Applied Wisdom podcast series for your corps members, alumni, and staff.

We start with one co-branded episode. Episode 17 is already built. If it lands well, we can produce additional co-branded episodes, potentially tailoring one to each of your key audiences. City Year shares the content through your existing channels. Your community gets leadership content connected to your brand.

The Review Process

From Topic to Landing Page

Every episode follows the same clear process. City Year's input shapes the brief, and the brief drives everything else.

  1. Topic Selection: Together, we choose an episode topic from the 66-episode series that fits City Year's audience and priorities.
  2. Brief Development: We create an episode brief that frames the topic through City Year's lens. This is where City Year gives input and direction: tone, emphasis, what to highlight, what to avoid. See the Episode 17 brief →
  3. Brief Approval: City Year reviews and approves the brief. This is the go/no-go decision point. Once approved, the brief becomes the source material for everything that follows.
  4. Asset Production: From the approved brief, we generate the complete asset suite: video overview, deep-dive podcast, slides, infographic, mind map, and transcript. No additional input needed from City Year at this stage.
  5. Content Review: City Year reviews the finished assets. If the tone or emphasis needs adjustment, we can regenerate with your direction. One focused round.
  6. Live: The co-branded landing page goes live with the full asset suite. City Year shares it with your audience.
